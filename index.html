FM Transceiver Application

A desktop application that receives and transmits FM radio signals, with user-configurable parameters for tuning and signal characteristics. Built in Proteus 8 Professional as part of the Analog Communications lab at LIU — it turned a fixed lab bench exercise into something I could actually experiment with, letting me change transmission parameters and observe the effect on the received signal without rewiring hardware each time.

AI Document Assistant

A PDF analysis tool that runs a local model through Ollama to summarize documents and answer questions about their contents. Built with Python, Streamlit for the interface, and PyPDF2 to extract the document text. The problem it solves: searching a long technical PDF with Ctrl+F only finds exact words, and cloud AI tools mean uploading documents you may not want to send anywhere — running the model locally keeps the files on your machine.

About Me

I'm Rimah Boukarroum, a Computer and Communications Engineering student based in Beirut, Lebanon. My work sits at the intersection of networking and software development, with a strong interest in signal processing, embedded systems, and communication technologies.
